Audio in Fatal Contact: what plays, where it comes from, and the automatic gates every shipped sound must pass.

## Rules

Every sound in `assets/audio` is generated by `tools/make_ui_audio.py` (synthesis plus the flite `rms` voice for announcer lines, MIT / BSD flite licence, see `licence.json`). Nothing is copied or derived from the unlicensed Quake sound packs. Any other sound added later must be generated or verified CC0, with provenance in `licence.json`.

## Gates (`tools/audio_check.py`)

`python3 tools/audio_check.py [--spec DIR] [paths]` exits 1 on any failure. It checks per category: integrated LUFS range, true peak below -1 dBFS, no clipped samples, at most 10 ms of leading silence, spectral centroid ceiling, tonal-peak ratio ceiling (no pure ringing tones), and tail length. `--spec DIR` renders spectrogram PNGs to inspect.

| Category | Examples | LUFS | Centroid max | Tail max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hit | hit_body, hit_head, hit_armor, hit_kill | -26..-16 | low-mid | 0.4 s |
| announcer | the 16 lines | -24..-12 | about 2 kHz | 1.1 s |
| move, impact, ui, pickup, ambience | later slices | defined in the script | | |

## Announcer

`game/fc_announcer.gd` queues lines: highest priority first, one at a time with a gap after each, lines older than the stale time are dropped. Settings (0 = off, no caps): `announce_<line>` is the streak or multi-kill count that triggers the line, `announce_gap` and `announce_stale` are seconds.
